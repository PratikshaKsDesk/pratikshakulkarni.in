Project

Mototrek

Mototrek is built around two verticals — adventure experiences and a riding gear store.

The work involved building the brand from scratch and handling both verticals across digital and offline touchpoints.

Understanding

The brand needed to connect with riders on two levels — experiences and products.

The challenge was to maintain a clear identity while handling both adventure and store offerings.

Approach

The focus was to build a strong and consistent brand across both verticals.

Communication, visuals, and tone needed to feel aligned while still supporting different use cases.

Execution

Designed the complete brand identity including logo and visual direction.

Built the website and landing pages for different offerings.

Set up social media and created content for both adventure and store segments.

Worked on offline event creatives and communication to support on-ground activities.

My Role

I worked on building and managing the brand across multiple areas.

This included branding, website design, content writing, social media setup, and marketing execution.

I also worked on defining the brand tone and ensuring consistency across both verticals.

Outcome

• Brand identity created for a multi-vertical business

• Website and landing pages developed

• Social media presence established and managed

• Consistent brand tone across platforms

• Offline event creatives designed

• Structured communication for both adventure and store
