Project

Maan India

This project focuses on helping women artisans turn their skills into something sustainable.

The idea was to simplify how they showcase, sell, and manage their work without depending on complex systems.

Understanding

Women artisans already have strong skills.

The challenge is not capability. It is lack of access, structure, and a clear way to reach buyers.

Approach

Instead of building a complex marketplace, the focus was on creating a simple system they can actually use.

Everything was planned around ease of use and clarity.

Execution

The platform was structured to allow easy product listing, order handling, and communication.

Supporting layers like logistics and payments were also considered to make the system complete.

My Role

I worked on understanding the problem, defining how the system should work, and shaping the product accordingly.

This included research, product direction, and structuring the experience for low-tech users.

Current State

The platform is currently in development.

The focus is on building something that is simple to use and can scale as more artisans join.
