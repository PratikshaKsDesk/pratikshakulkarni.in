Project

E-Learning Platform

This project focuses on making skill-based learning more accessible and easier to follow.

The idea was to connect learners with the right experts and create a structured learning experience.

Understanding

Learning options are available, but they are either too rigid or too scattered.

Learners find it difficult to choose the right expert, and experts don’t always have a simple way to teach and manage their content.

Approach

The focus was to create a system that feels structured but still flexible.

Everything needed to be simple to use and easy to follow for both learners and instructors.

Execution

The platform was planned with structured courses, live sessions, and supporting content like notes and discussions.

The aim was to make the learning process clear and organized instead of scattered.

My Role

I worked on understanding the problem, defining how the platform should function, and structuring the overall experience.

This included research, user flow planning, and shaping how learning and teaching would work together.

Current State

The platform is in development.

The focus is on building something that is easy to use and can grow as more users join.
